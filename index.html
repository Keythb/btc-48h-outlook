Spot, funding, implied and realized volatility are sourced live in the browser from public Deribit, Coinbase Exchange, and alternative.me endpoints; option open interest is a once-daily snapshot from Deribit (BTC options) and Schwab market data (IBIT options). Implied volatility is Deribit's mark IV for the at-the-money option nearest 30 days to expiry; realized volatility is the annualized standard deviation of the last 30 daily log returns of Coinbase BTC-USD closes. σ levels use a log-normal model and are estimates, not forecasts. Put/call walls, max pain, and the IBIT-to-BTC conversion (strike × BTC/IBIT price ratio at snapshot time) are model-based and subject to data error. Data may be delayed or incomplete.
